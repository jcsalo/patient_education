## Metastatic Gastric Cancer

#### Slide content

Systemic Therapy:
Chemotherapy
Immunotherapy

The primary treatment for metastatic gastric cancer is systemic therapy. These are drugs that can circulate throughout the bloodstream.

There are two types of systemic therapy:
Chemotherapy are drugs which directly kill cancer calls
Immunotherapy are drugs which stimulate your body’s immune system to kill cancer cells
Most systemic therapy is given intravenously
